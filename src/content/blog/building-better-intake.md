Good intake starts before a meeting is booked. Ask for the context that changes the conversation, then give people a short path to the next step.

## Start with the decision

Write down what you need to decide after the form is complete. Keep only the questions that help make that decision. A focused form feels faster and gives your team better signal.

## Connect the handoff

When a response needs a conversation, send people directly to the right booking link. LinkyCal keeps the form and scheduling step in one flow, so the next action is clear.
