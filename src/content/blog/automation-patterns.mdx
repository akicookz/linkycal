Teams usually get the most value from automation when each workflow has one clear trigger and one useful next action.

## 1. Route qualified requests

Use a form response as the trigger, then route qualified requests to a booking link. Keep the first workflow small enough that someone can understand it at a glance.

## 2. Keep contacts useful

Tag a contact when they submit a form or book a meeting. That gives the team a shared view without asking anyone to maintain another spreadsheet.
